What the repository gets right

It is worth stating plainly, because the criticism is easier to write: this project's build is idempotent, its gate genuinely runs, its official-data lineage checks out row by row, its only graded result is correct, and it publishes its own failures instead of hiding them. Most projects that claim to originate lines do none of those things.

The three findings that change a decision

The headline is stronger than the statistics

S1's p = 0.020 is a raw, uncorrected p-value from one of six tests. Corrected, q = 0.120. Its win rate barely responds to edge size (r = 0.051). The site's most confident sentence is the least defensible one.

F-05 · question Q-03

The injury layer is a day behind on a game day

All ten teams filed between 04:15 and 13:30 ET. Twenty-two designations moved. The published layer still shows the morning snapshot and does not say it is a snapshot.

F-03, F-04 · Q-04

Rest is computed wrongly and two pages disagree

26 of 27 horizon rows store a negative rest value because the "previous game" is taken from a fixture list that includes future dates. Game flow and picks then contradict each other.
