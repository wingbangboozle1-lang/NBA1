Why there is no odds source here

No NBA-operated source publishes a sportsbook price, and no licensed price feed is connected to this repository. That is not an oversight in this session's research — it is the central open question of the project (Q-01) and the reason every "edge" number on the main site is model-versus-model rather than model-versus-market. A sportsbook's price would also not be an official source in the sense this site uses the word, which is why this register contains none.
