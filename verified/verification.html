The captures behind these rows

Method

  • Official only. Values are confirmed against www.nba.com, official.nba.com or ak-static.cms.nba.com. No sportsbook, aggregator or news site was used for any value.
  • Exact match. A value is verified only if it agrees to the published precision. Where a page rounds (e.g. PPG to one decimal) the comparison is made at that precision.
  • Editions are part of the claim. An injury designation is recorded with the edition stamp, because designations move during a game day.
  • Two reads that disagree make a value unverified. Cooper Flagg's average returned 21.0 and 24.6 on two reads of the same official page, so it is published as unverified (V-09), not averaged.
  • Recomputation is not verification. Where a row says VERIFIED for a statistic it means the arithmetic reproduces exactly from the data the repository stores — it does not mean the underlying scores were re-read. The column on the right is the honest per-row distinction.
