Executive summary

The repository's engineering is sound and reproducible, its official-data lineage checks out row by row, and its strongest performance claim does not survive a multiple-testing correction — while the one layer that changes fastest during a game day, injuries, is fifteen hours stale at first tip.

1. What this session did

  1. Claimed its own scope on the coordination board and pushed the claim before touching anything (COORDINATION.md §30).
  2. Read 19 official NBA pages in this session and compared them, row by row, with what the repository publishes: 28 verification rows covering 3,873 compared values.
  3. Re-graded the only graded result the project has (the 2026-11-13 slate) against the official finals: 9 of 9 scores match, and the published 3-4 record reproduces exactly.
  4. Recomputed every published statistic that can be recomputed without a market price: exact binomial p-values, Wilson bounds, the luck decomposition, 13 correlation coefficients, and the model's error statistics.
  5. Found five defects that change decisions (F-01 prop lines stale, F-03/F-04 injury layer stale, F-05 headline overstated, F-12 rest field wrong) and ten housekeeping items.
  6. Wrote the decision layer that was missing: this summary, 12 questions with the step that answers each, and 12 options for someone to choose between.

3. What is not true as published

Each of these is a statement the repository makes. The official read does not support it in the form it is published. The full reasoning and the fix are on Repo review and Flagged.

"For the first time one strategy has cleared the luck gate"

True as a raw p-value, false as a skill claim. Across the six strategies that were tested on the same 60 games, Benjamini-Hochberg puts S1's q at 0.120 — it does not survive correction. And S1's win rate barely improves as its edge grows (11/16 on the smallest disagreements, 12/16 on the largest; r = 0.051), which is not what a real edge looks like.

instead: One strategy is nominally ahead after 32 picks and is unproven. It should be pre-registered, not funded.
flag: F-05

"Picks are generated for every upcoming slate using our originated lines" with totals and player props

Totals and prop lines are not market lines and not originated numbers of the same kind as the spread. The prop 'line' is the player's own season average — sometimes one game stale — so three of the four props re-checked have a side that is flattered or reversed by the stale number.

instead: Spreads are originated from a stated formula. Totals and props are projections compared against a non-market reference. The three should be labelled differently, because they are.
flag: F-01, Q-07

"Five of the ten teams playing on 11/14 have still not filed"

True of the 04:15 AM edition the page cites, false as of the 1:30 PM edition: all ten teams had filed, and 22 designations had changed.

instead: The injury layer is 15 hours stale on a game day and the page does not say so.
flag: F-03, F-04

Rest and back-to-back context is published for every horizon game

The horizon builder computes 'days since the previous game' against a fixture list that includes future dates, so 26 of 27 rows store a negative value, and the game-flow page and the picks page disagree with each other about the same game.

instead: There is no trustworthy rest field in the project today.
flag: F-12

"Every table row links to the official source it came from"

78 of 978 data rows carry no official link under an independent parse. The build's own audit counts rows under a narrower definition.

instead: Most rows are source-linked; the sentence needs the definition attached.
flag: F-10

6. What to decide

Options are laid out on Suggestions with effort, risk and a definition of done, so one path can be chosen before anything is built.

Is this a market-beating project or a model-quality project?

Licensed price feed (expensive, unlocks the real test) or a written 'model quality only' statement (free, honest, ends the ambiguity).

why_now: Nothing can be called validated until this is answered; every gate that needs a price is blocked.

Fund S1, pre-register it, or drop it?

Pre-register and wait for 250-300 picks (recommended); or publish the corrected q-value and keep it at quarter size; or remove it from the leaderboard.

why_now: The site's headline invites a funding decision the statistics do not support.

Which build is the product?

Root site/ (recommended: it is the only build whose gate passes and whose output is reproducible), or the Integration Desk.

why_now: Six codebases, one origin, contradictory numbers. Every fix costs five times as much until this is settled.

Does the injury layer become automated before the next slate?

Wire the existing refresh workflow to ingest the newest edition and publish the diff (2-3 days), or mark the injury layer as a snapshot and stop presenting it as current.

why_now: It is the fastest-moving input and currently the stalest part of the site.

7. What is still unknown

  • Whether any strategy beats a market: untestable today, no price source exists.
  • Whether S1 is real: needs about 250-300 graded picks and a pre-registered rule.
  • How much a designation like 'Questionable' actually costs in minutes: never measured.
  • Which rho values are right for parlay legs: five of them are asserted, none measured for a player leg.
  • Whether the 45 unre-read final scores are correct: completeness is proven, correctness is not.
  • What rights the project has to publish derived NBA content: not stated anywhere in the repository.
