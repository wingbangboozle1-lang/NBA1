Where the site overstates itself

Three of the repository's load-bearing statements do not survive an official read. Each one is developed in full on Repo review and listed with its fix on Flagged.

"For the first time one strategy has cleared the luck gate"

True as a raw p-value, false as a skill claim. Across the six strategies that were tested on the same 60 games, Benjamini-Hochberg puts S1's q at 0.120 — it does not survive correction. And S1's win rate barely improves as its edge grows (11/16 on the smallest disagreements, 12/16 on the largest; r = 0.051), which is not what a real edge looks like.

Instead: One strategy is nominally ahead after 32 picks and is unproven. It should be pre-registered, not funded. · F-05

"Picks are generated for every upcoming slate using our originated lines" with totals and player props

Totals and prop lines are not market lines and not originated numbers of the same kind as the spread. The prop 'line' is the player's own season average — sometimes one game stale — so three of the four props re-checked have a side that is flattered or reversed by the stale number.

Instead: Spreads are originated from a stated formula. Totals and props are projections compared against a non-market reference. The three should be labelled differently, because they are. · F-01, Q-07

"Five of the ten teams playing on 11/14 have still not filed"

True of the 04:15 AM edition the page cites, false as of the 1:30 PM edition: all ten teams had filed, and 22 designations had changed.

Instead: The injury layer is 15 hours stale on a game day and the page does not say so. · F-03, F-04

What needs your decision

Four decisions are blocking everything else. Each links to the question that has to be settled first and to the options on Suggestions.

Is this a market-beating project or a model-quality project?

Licensed price feed (expensive, unlocks the real test) or a written 'model quality only' statement (free, honest, ends the ambiguity).

Nothing can be called validated until this is answered; every gate that needs a price is blocked. · Q-01

Fund S1, pre-register it, or drop it?

Pre-register and wait for 250-300 picks (recommended); or publish the corrected q-value and keep it at quarter size; or remove it from the leaderboard.

The site's headline invites a funding decision the statistics do not support. · Q-03

Which build is the product?

Root site/ (recommended: it is the only build whose gate passes and whose output is reproducible), or the Integration Desk.

Six codebases, one origin, contradictory numbers. Every fix costs five times as much until this is settled. · Q-06

Does the injury layer become automated before the next slate?

Wire the existing refresh workflow to ingest the newest edition and publish the diff (2-3 days), or mark the injury layer as a snapshot and stop presenting it as current.

It is the fastest-moving input and currently the stalest part of the site. · Q-04
