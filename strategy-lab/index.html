What this directory is. The rest of the site reports lines, picks and gates on a 51-game window, because that is the only window a point-in-time team rating was ever captured for. The repository already holds a bigger official base: 160 scored games, every one transcribed from nba.com/games. This lab re-runs the audit on all of it with a walk-forward that cannot see the future, and publishes what survives. It reads committed files and writes only inside strategy-lab/ — no other session's data moved while it was running.

Findings

1. The game log was 23 games short, and the fix can only be a schedule fix

Three official instruments now agree: 23 of 23 fixture rows match the copy already in the repository, 29 of 29 dates match the official per-date count, and 30 of 30 teams match the official games-played column. The 23 results are still unpublished, so no score was filled in.

2. The rest layer never read the fixtures its own file promises it

46 of 366 team-slots change when the fixtures enter the history, and 2 back-to-back flags flip. None of them falls inside the 51-game window the leaderboard was graded on, so no published pick changes today; what it does invalidate is any October backtest, which is the base this lab used.

the table behind it →

3. Nothing in a 13-strategy library beats the originated line

The largest excess over the model's own expectation is 3.84 wins on 58 plays (z = 1.14, exact p = 0.15963, Benjamini-Hochberg q = 0.6394). A shared-draw simulation of the whole library reaches a best that large 100% of the time when every strategy is pure noise. That is the luck removal asked for, and the answer is that the signal is not there yet.

4. The back-to-back penalty is a one-week fit applied to every line

The site carries 6.096 points. Measured on 144 games where both teams have a game on record it is 0.016 ± 4.426 (p = 0.9971). On the current slate 11 of 27 games move by 3 points or more between those two choices.

5. The published probability columns are close, except on overtime

Mean published home-win probability 0.5467 against a realised 0.525; margin inside 5 0.1888 against 0.1812; overtime 0.0191 predicted against 0.05 realised (8 of 160 games, exact p = 0.01213).

Method, in the order it is executed

1 · Repair the base

Re-read the two missing dates from the official day pages, reconcile the log against the official per-date count and the official games-played column, and record what the pages do not publish instead of filling it in.

2 · Recompute the layers that depend on it

Rest days, back-to-back flags and team form are rebuilt on the complete schedule and diffed against what is shipped, slot by slot.

3 · Grade against the right null

Each play is graded against the probability the originated line itself implied. A raw hit rate is published too, and labelled as the thing that proves nothing.

4 · Correct for looking

Benjamini-Hochberg across the library, a shared-draw best-of-library simulation, a moving-block bootstrap on the units, and a both-halves stability requirement.
