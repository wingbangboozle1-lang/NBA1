The reward rule, in one sentence. A strategy earns a stake only if it wins more often than the originated line it bets against already implies, on at least 30 plays, in both halves of the window, with a multiple-comparison correction across the whole library — and the market gate is reserved for a price feed this repository does not have. Nothing here passes it, so every tier below is paper.
