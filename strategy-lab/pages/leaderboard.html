What a play is graded against. Each play is graded against the probability the originated line itself implied. A strategy earns promotion only by winning MORE often than its own model said it would - which is the question a bookmaker would ask and the one a repository with no odds feed can still answer. A raw hit rate against 52.38% is deliberately not the headline: betting a strong favourite straight up clears that bar for free and proves nothing.
Walk-forward discipline. all parameters and both team ratings use only games strictly earlier than the game being predicted Window 2026-10-20 .. 2026-11-13: 160 scored games, 94 of them gradeable (both teams need 4 games on record first).
