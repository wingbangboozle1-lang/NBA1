Questions & suggestions

A decision-ready checklist of knowns, unknowns, and next steps. Answers are limited to facts supported by the repository or official NBA sources. Missing answers are not guessed.

Autonomous work boundary.This session can document the architecture and build the static site. It cannot invent historical results, provide credentials, select a commercial odds provider or legal jurisdiction, or truthfully claim live ingestion/deployment without those systems and permissions. The suggestions below define a safe default path and clearly flag decisions requiring accountable review.

Default decisions suggested for a safe first release

  • Use no user-entered data, but do not imply missing upstream data exists: fail closed and show “unavailable.”
  • Allow a slate display only for officially confirmed games with a timestamped source snapshot and a rolling maximum 72-hour horizon.
  • Keep every pick disabled until provenance, market-price access, test gates, and shadow testing are complete.
  • Do not show a numerical correlation or injury minute delta without its sample, source, model version, and uncertainty.
  • Maintain this suggestions section as a decision log; record a reviewer, choice, date, rationale, and files affected before implementation.
