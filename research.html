Model design

Strategy lab

Candidate signals, dependency checks, and release gates. All numeric estimates remain blank until computed from reproducible, time-stamped data. These are research proposals—not picks or claims of edge.

Principle: separate prediction from selection. First estimate the event probability from information available at decision time. Then compare with a valid market price and account for price, settlement rules, and uncertainty. A correct prediction at a bad price can still be a bad wager; no market data is connected here.

Leaderboard design

  • Only gate-passing frozen strategies are eligible; show sample size and uncertainty beside every rank.
  • Rank on a predeclared, risk-adjusted and price-aware score; disclose drawdown, calibration, coverage and exposure concentration.
  • Keep singles, props and parlays in separate cohorts. Do not reward volume or count correlated legs as independent evidence.
  • Preserve all predictions, quotes, model versions and outcomes—including losing, void and skipped picks.

Awarding prizes or staking funds is a product/business decision requiring compliance review. No rewards mechanism is active.
