Luck Decomposition

Which records are ahead of or behind what the teams' own point differentials imply
as of …

A team's win-loss record is a noisy measurement of how good it is. Pythagorean expectation converts a team's official offensive and defensive ratings into the win rate its point differential implies; the gap between that and its actual wins is the part that came from close-game outcomes. A large gap is not proof of luck, but it is a reason not to price a record.

i

What this is and is not. Expected wins come from the official OffRtg and DefRtg with a fixed exponent (13.91). The z-score divides the gap by the binomial standard deviation over games played. Nothing here is a prediction.

"Luck" is a statistical description of the gap between wins and point differential — not a claim that a team is fortunate in a moral sense, and not a forecast. Regression to the mean is the base case, not a certainty.
