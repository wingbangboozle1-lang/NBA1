NBA1 Command Center

Originated lines · gated strategies · correlations · luck · injuries & game flow
as of …

An independent view of the NBA built only from official league data. Lines are originated here, not copied from a book; every strategy has to clear seven gates before it is allowed to matter; and every number on every page links to the official page it came from. Where a number cannot be confirmed it is left blank and put on the Flagged for Review page.

!

Verdict: provisional — nothing is funded. The model beats the naive reference on margin accuracy, loses to it on totals and on probability calibration, and the best strategy's 16-7 record is still well inside what luck produces on 23 graded plays (exact binomial p = 0.093).

This is the honest read, not a marketing line. The executive summary and the validation page show the arithmetic behind it.

What this build does not do

•

No licensed sportsbook price is used. The "reference line" is an internal naive model, so an edge shown here is an edge against a construction, never against a book. No player minutes are projected. No correlation between bets and results is measured. No play is settled, so no ROI is claimed. This is a research artefact, not a betting service.
