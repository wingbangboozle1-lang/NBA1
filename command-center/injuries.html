Injuries

Every official status on the 19:30 ET report, with minutes-coverage confidence beside every impact figure
as of …

Transcribed row for row from the official NBA injury report, with the report's own edition time. A status is what a team published about participation — not a forecast of minutes, usage or production.

!

Read the confidence column before the impact column. The impact number needs a player's minutes. Minutes are only known for the 50 players in the captured official player table, so for everyone else the impact is counted as zero and the row says so. The figure is therefore a lower bound, and a team with a low minutes-coverage percentage has a near-meaningless impact number.

An absent entry is not a healthy team. Where a team had not submitted a report, the row reads "NOT YET SUBMITTED", exactly as published.
