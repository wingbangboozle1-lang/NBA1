A decision cannot be reasonable while a material question is unanswered. This register separates questions the data on hand can already answer from those it cannot, and for every gap it names the exact artefact that would close it and what decision is blocked until it does.

i

Reading rule. ANSWERED means the fact is in this repository and traceable to an official URL. PARTIAL means one side is evidence-backed and the other is missing, and both are shown. OPEN means nothing here can answer it yet. A blank is a gap, never a zero.
