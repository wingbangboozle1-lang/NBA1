No prop probability is published anywhere in this build. A baseline is an expectation, not a distribution: without game-to-game variance from official player game logs, any "over 23.5 points" probability would be invented. So the site shows the baseline, the adjustment, and says what is missing.

Every number here can be checked by hand: projected = baseline × pace × defence × usage, and the three multipliers are printed in their own columns. Baseline is the player's official per-game average; pace is the game's expected possessions over his team's season pace; defence is the opponent's published defensive rating against the league mean; usage is the vacancy left by a teammate the report lists as out (1.000 means no adjustment was made — including when a report had not been submitted, which is not the same as a healthy roster). There is no probability column because a probability needs game-to-game variance, and only season averages are in this repository.
