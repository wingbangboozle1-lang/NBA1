What is measured on official data, and what is only modelled — kept apart on purpose

Two different things share the word "correlation" here and conflating them is the usual way a parlay gets mispriced. Measured means computed from official NBA data with a stated sample. Model-derived means assumed, and every parlay that depends on it is labelled accordingly.

!

No same-game-parlay rho in this build is measured. Measuring leg dependence needs paired bet outcome data, which does not exist in this repository. The five rho values below are assumptions with a stated direction and a plausible size — not estimates.

Parlay pricing grid

How the assumed rho moves a two-leg parlay's joint probability away from the independence shortcut, holding both leg probabilities fixed. A positive rho reduces the joint probability of two overs, which is why multiplying marginals is the wrong default even when each leg looks good. Prices are fair American prices with no vig, so they are what the joint probability is worth — not what a book would offer. The last column is the edge a bettor gets if a book prices the two legs as independent: negative means the independent price is the generous one.
