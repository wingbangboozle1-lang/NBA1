Proposed operating loop

  1. Snapshot official schedule, stats and injury reports with as-of timestamps.
  2. Estimate minutes and production as a distribution, not a single “replacement” number.
  3. Generate probability distributions and fair lines; compare only against timestamped, licensed odds.
  4. Validate with rolling, time-ordered holdouts and exposure-aware baselines.
  5. Publish only gate-passing candidates; score fairly on a risk-adjusted leaderboard.

Design recommendations, not verified claims of performance. Full test plan is in Strategy Lab.

Decision posture

Keep picks disabled until the data, market, evaluation, and governance gaps on the Flagged for Review page are resolved. Do not treat raw season averages, injury labels, or a handful of wins as evidence of skill.
