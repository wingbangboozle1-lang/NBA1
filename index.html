What this site is, and what it is not

This is an origination and research workspace. It produces its own lines from official NBA data and then tries hard to prove that those lines carry skill. As of this build no strategy has cleared the skill gate: the best one is 23-9 at Tier 3 - Quarter unit, which is inside what luck alone produces. Every strategy is therefore capped at a quarter unit or held back as research-only, and that is the result, not an obstacle to be worked around.

No sportsbook price is used anywhere, because no NBA-operated source publishes one. “Edge” on this site means the model against a deliberately naive reference model built from the same official ratings. It does not mean an edge against a book.

Executive summary

Every number on this site that comes from an official NBA page was re-read from that page on 2026-11-14 between 18:40Z and 19:10Z, and the result is: the horizon is right, the injury data was stale, and the game log is short by a measurable and uniform amount. All 27 games in the 14-17 November window match official nba.com field for field, and all 54 printed team records agree with a second independent official source. But the site's injury pages were built from the 11/13 07:30 PM ET edition while the official index already carried the 11/14 01:30 PM ET game-day edition, in which four of the five teams that had not filed overnight had filed. And the official game log reconciles to 160 games against the standings' 183 - exactly 23 games short, uniformly across all 30 teams. None of those three findings changes a single published pick, line or gate result. All three change what a reader is entitled to conclude from them.

This is a verification and decision-reading of the whole site, not a rebuild. The pipeline that originates the lines, tests the strategies and settles the picks was left exactly as it was: changing it under cover of a verification pass is how an audit trail stops being one. What changed is the evidence base - a fresh official capture of the horizon, the finals and the newest injury edition - and what was added is the comparison of that evidence against what the site already says. The single honest headline is unchanged from the previous build and is not softened here: no strategy is funded, one has cleared the luck gate and then failed calibration and stability, and no market price exists anywhere in the repository, so no result here is a claim about profit.

Verdict — PROVISIONAL - not funded, and now verified against a fresher official read
The model still beats the naive reference on margin accuracy (MAE 12.98 against 13.62 on the 60-game out-of-sample window) and still loses on totals (14.83 against 14.72) and on picking winners (42-18 reference, 40-20 model). S1 is still 23-9 with p = 0.020 and a Wilson lower bound of 0.546 against a 0.524 break-even, and still fails calibration (0.719 hit rate against 0.627 predicted) and stability, and still cannot run the availability gate. It stays capped at a quarter unit. The fresh official read does not move any of those numbers - it confirms the inputs they were computed from are current.
Why: Because the alternative is the thing this project was built to avoid. A verification pass that quietly upgraded a quarter-unit strategy to funded on the strength of 32 graded plays would be exactly the luck-as-skill error the gates exist to catch. The gates did not move, because there was no new evidence to move them: the same 60 games, the same ratings window, the same ten strategies. What the fresh read adds is confidence in the inputs, not in the conclusions.
  1. The horizon is exactly right, and it was checked against two official sources per record — 27 of 27 published horizon rows matched the official day pages on date, ET tip time, matchup and game id; per-day counts match the official calendar badges (5 / 9 / 8 / 5); and all 54 team-record cells agree with the standings page as well as the game card. The 11/13 finals also re-verified 9 of 9, including the ATL@CHI overtime label.
    This is the strongest statement the site can currently make: not 'we believe this is right' but 'two independent official pages say the same thing, and the ids match'. It is also a snapshot at 18:45Z - the 11/14 games tip at 7:00 PM ET.
  2. The injury data was more than twelve hours stale, and the site's own label did not say so loudly enough — The official index carried the full 11/14 game-day series from 12:00 AM ET to 01:30 PM ET. In the 01:30 PM ET edition, New Orleans (5 rows), Cleveland (8), Chicago (7) and Miami (4) had all filed; only Memphis had not, and Memphis was still inside its own 1 p.m. CT window. The published site was still showing those teams as NOT YET SUBMITTED from the 04:15 AM ET edition.
    Four of the five 'unavailable' teams were not unavailable, they were unlisted. That is the single most consequential finding of this pass for anyone reading the Injuries page tonight, and no pick changes because of it: the injury module does not move a line for a designation it cannot measure.
  3. The game log is short by exactly 23 games, uniformly, and that is now proven rather than suspected — Total points for and against in the log balance exactly (36,240), and wins sum to the 160 games, so the log is internally consistent. Against the standings it holds 320 team-games to the standings' 366: 46 team-games, i.e. 23 games. Fourteen teams are short exactly 1, sixteen exactly 2, none short 0, none short 3 or more. Wins and losses are short by 23 each.
    Because the shortfall is uniform, team-to-team comparisons on this window survive it, and the luck table published alongside this summary is usable for ranking. Calendar-complete rest is not, and every rest figure that crosses the two row-less dates is labelled a lower bound. The missing games are not named, because no official page prints their scores and the standings residual would be inference.
  4. Luck is measurable on the official record, and the first surprise is that close games are rare — On 160 official finals the mean absolute margin is 14.06 points; only 13 games (8.1%) were decided by 3 or fewer, while 57 (35.6%) were decided by 15 or more. Home teams went 84-76. A Pythagorean expectation gives a luck-wins spread of +2.72 (Philadelphia) to -2.36 (New York).
    The intuition that NBA results are mostly coin flips is wrong at this sample: blowouts dominate, and the luck that exists is concentrated in which team got the blowouts. That is why a 23-9 record at a 0.52 break-even can be statistically real and still not be skill - the wins have to come from games that were actually in doubt.
  5. Single bets, parlays and prop combos are not the same product, and the data says which is which — A single bet needs its edge to survive one variance term. A parlay needs every leg to survive one, and the measured same-game pairwise correlation in points is 0.82 - much higher than independence - so same-game legs compound in a direction the pricing intuition usually gets backwards. Props are the thinnest of the three: only 10 of 88 rows have a measured dispersion, and none of those 10 clears one measured SD against the player's own season average (the median gap is 0.16 SD).
    The product ranking that the evidence supports is single bets first, cross-game parlays second and conditional, same-game combos third, props last until a measured line exists. The site previously implied this; it now states it with the measured correlations and the unpriced count attached.
  6. A team's rotation minutes are still unknown for most of the league, so the injury impact stays a lower bound — The official per-game minutes capture holds ranks 1-50 sorted by points. Of the 51 player rows listed for the five 11/14 games, only a small minority have any measured minutes in a committed official capture; every other row contributes a null, never a zero, and the page prints the coverage per team next to the figure.
    This is the binding constraint on the whole injury story. Until it is fixed, any 'injury moves the line by X' claim is scaling a null, and the site is right to refuse to do it - which is also why finding 2 does not change any pick.
  7. The back-to-back layer is exact for this horizon and lower-bounded only at its very start — Derived from official dates across 2026-10-20 to 2026-11-17: five team-slots are on a back-to-back on 11/14 (NOP, CLE, MIA, MEM, MIN) and none is on 11/15-11/17. The only place rest can be understated is a figure that spans 2026-10-24 or 2026-11-02, the two dates with no rows in the official log, and every such cell says so.
    A date fact beats a model: either two official dates are one day apart or they are not. This is the cheapest trustworthy signal in the whole system, and it is worth more than most of the expensive ones.

NBA1 — a research workspace for originating NBA lines and testing whether the signal is skill or luck. Nothing on this site is a betting recommendation, and no market price is used anywhere: every "edge" is against a naive model that stands in for the market, because no official NBA-operated price feed exists.
