What this site is, and what it is not

This is an origination and research workspace. It produces its own lines from official NBA data and then tries hard to prove that those lines carry skill. As of this build no strategy is funded. The best one — 23-8 at Tier 3 - Quarter unit — has cleared the registered luck gate G4 (Wilson 95% lower bound on its hit rate above the 52.38% break-even; the exact binomial p = 0.020 is uncorrected for the number of strategies tried, and this gate set registers no multiplicity gate), but it fails the calibration and stability gates and the availability gate cannot run, so it stays capped at a quarter unit. Every strategy is therefore capped at a quarter unit or held back as research-only, and that is the result, not an obstacle to be worked around.

No sportsbook price is used anywhere, because no NBA-operated source publishes one. “Edge” on this site means the model against a deliberately naive reference model built from the same official ratings. It does not mean an edge against a book.

Two models, two different lines - which one is authoritative?

Every game below carries a line from the validated model (engine/pipeline.py, the one tested on 60 out-of-sample games). A second, independent line-origination model (RecordLine-1, scripts/originate_horizon.py) produces its own spread for the same games. They are different models, not two renderings of one number: on the 17 games they share they differ on 17, and on 2 of those the two disagree about which team is the favourite (largest gap 11.8 points).

The line shown on this page is the authoritative one because it is the only one whose error has been measured out of sample. Do not average the two and do not treat the second as a second opinion on the same estimate - it is a different, unmeasured model. The full row-by-row comparison is in Official Source Check, section 5. Choosing between them, or retiring one, is an open decision (flag F26, suggestion SG15).

SIGN CONVENTION (read this once): 'Model spread' is the projected home margin, i.e. home points minus away points. A NEGATIVE number means the home team is projected to LOSE by that many points. That is the opposite of the way a book prints a home spread, and the two must not be mixed up (flag F27). Spread and total are the originated lines (model). Edge is the model minus the naive reference line built from the same official ratings; positive spread edge means the model likes the home side more than the reference does.
