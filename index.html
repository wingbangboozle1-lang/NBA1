PROMOTION POLICY

Validation gates

A strategy stays in research until it clears every gate. Thresholds require explicit approval and historical evidence; none are presumed here.

01

Data integrity

Document source, timestamp, revisions, coverage, settlement definitions, and missingness. No post-event leakage.

Required before modeling
02

Out-of-sample design

Chronological train/validation/test; rolling-origin evaluation. Freeze features and rules before final holdout.

Required before scoring
03

Calibration & uncertainty

Measure probability calibration (e.g. reliability/Brier score) and uncertainty by market and probability band.

Report confidence intervals
04

Economic value

Compare with contemporaneous available prices, account for vig, pushes/voids, limits and realistic execution.

Net results, not hit rate
05

Robustness & multiplicity

Pre-register candidate tests; correct for multiple comparisons; stress-test seasons, teams and market regimes.

Penalize selection bias
06

Live shadow period

Log immutable pregame predictions before settlement. Require a pre-agreed sample and stability policy.

Promotion requires sign-off
Luck control: report uncertainty around ROI and calibration, benchmark against simple baselines and market prices, and avoid winner-only leaderboard rankings. A short-term positive run is not proof of skill.

PREGAME CONTEXT

Injuries & game flow

Availability is time-sensitive. A report is not a guarantee of participation, minutes, or production.

NO LIVE FEED

Rotation impact workflow

  1. Ingest official report with capture timestamp and status history.
  2. Confirm status updates close to tip; keep unknowns unknown.
  3. Build plausible rotation scenarios from prior lineup/minute evidence.
  4. Recompute minutes and production distributions, including uncertainty; never allocate absent-player minutes mechanically.
  5. Log assumptions and freeze each forecast version before the game.

Game flow checklist

  • Projected pace and possession range from dated team data.
  • Rotation depth and expected availability scenarios.
  • Foul, blowout, overtime, and role-change sensitivity.
  • Market movement only when timestamped prices are available.
  • Scenario range and confidence—not a single deterministic story.

Official injury report reference: NBA Communications — injury report page ↗. The page is season-specific and not a live slate feed. The official 11/13 5:15 PM ET report has been transcribed for this project on gameflow.html (impact context from printed values only; minutes remain unquantified).

HUMAN REVIEW BEFORE CHANGE

Suggestions

Proposed paths for review; these are not implemented decisions or validated findings.

REVIEW QUEUE
01 · FOUNDATION

Establish a versioned, point-in-time data pipeline

Start with official NBA schedules, box scores, player/team stats and dated injury reports; separately procure historically timestamped market prices with documented rights. Store source URL, retrieval time, game time, revision, and raw record checksum.

Decision owner: review data access and licensing before ingestion.
02 · VALIDATION

Freeze a pre-registered evaluation protocol

Set chronological holdouts, baseline comparisons, minimum sample/coverage, uncertainty reporting, multiple-testing controls and promotion thresholds before strategy search.

Decision owner: review criteria; no thresholds assumed here.
03 · AUTOMATION

Generate only evidence-qualified picks for a rolling 0–3 day horizon

Schedule refreshes, forecast versioning and injury-triggered recalculation. Suppress a pick when inputs are stale/missing or a gate fails; expose the reason and last-updated time.

Dependency: approved data feeds, uptime and fail-closed policy.
04 · GOVERNANCE

Make the leaderboard auditable and risk-aware

Show all eligible and retired methods, sample size, calibration, net performance with intervals, market coverage, methodology version and independent replay. Set rewards only after policy review.

Decision owner: define competition and responsible-use policy.
