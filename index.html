Method notes (verifiable)

Two estimators

Estimator A = additive efficiency model (ORTG/DRTG/pace, shrinkage). Estimator B = record-based (shrunk win% + measured HCA + rest). They are independent; their disagreement is the headline confidence signal. The blend is published underneath, never as the headline.

Home-court advantage

HCA = 1.09 points, pinned to the measured home-win rate (0.5333 over 60 official games). Not fitted freely. Source: NBA.com Games official scores.

Luck z-score per team

Pythagorean expected wins vs actual, z-scored by binomial SD. Teams outside the noise band (|z| ≥ 1.96) are refused for strategy use. Source: NBA.com Team Advanced.

Total model

Total = possessions × points-per-possession (official pace × official OffRtg), not an average of team scoring averages. Source: NBA.com Team Advanced.

Parlay pricing

Every parlay priced with measured rho (0.15), with the naive independence product printed beside it. Correlation is never hidden.

Injury banner

The Injuries page shows both the report revision time and the retrieval time, so readers know how stale the data is.
