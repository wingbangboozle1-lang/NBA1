Second-generation origination (engine/model_v2.py, rendered by scripts/build_horizon_page_v2.py): the home-court advantage is measured, not assumed; two independent official-input estimators publish their disagreement as the headline confidence signal; minutes redistribution, rest, back-to-back and travel from sourced arena coordinates are explicit; the market-anchored prior is built and labelled inert. Deterministic: rerunning the builder reproduces every number below byte-for-byte (run timestamps aside).

Read this first. Method & provenance

  • Model: engine/model_v2.py — bivariate-normal CDF validated against independent midpoint integration to ≤ 6e-6.
  • Lines: scripts/build_horizon_v2.py → data/derived/horizon_lines_v2.json; picks → horizon_picks_v2.json; report → model_report_v2.json.
  • Schedule: official 38-game schedule from nba.com/schedule and the per-date games pages; six tip conflicts published, one anomaly (AN-1) recorded.
  • Team ratings: the three official team-stat pages re-read 2026-11-15 — advanced, traditional, opponent.
  • Standings/HCA: official standings (HOME/ROAD columns, 188 home games, 102 home wins).
  • Minutes: official MIN-sorted leaderboard, page 1 (rows 1–50) captured; pages 2–10 unreachable (URL Page parameter not honored).
  • Injuries: official injury-report index, 06:15 PM ET 11/15 edition transcribed verbatim (155 rows) with the computed consecutive-edition diff.
  • Arena coordinates: sourced coordinate table (venue names official; coordinates secondary-source, flagged in the file).
