DESK-build_edge_site.pyHIGH`SIGMA = 11.0` is the scale that turns a projected margin into a win probability, fed through a logistic link. It was suspected of making every probability on lines.html too confident. Tested, that turns out to be the wrong diagnosis — and the real defect underneath it is worse for the site's credibility than a mis-set constant.Measured on the same 51 finals (integration/out/transform_test.json): at σ=11 the logistic link scores Brier 0.1943 and a normal link 0.1884, while the measured error σ of 17.4 scores 0.1937 — so σ=11 is close to optimal and the *link function* is what costs accuracy. What does not survive measurement is the home-advantage assumption: every variant puts mean P(home) between 0.512 and 0.566 while home teams actually won 25 of 51 (0.4902).
