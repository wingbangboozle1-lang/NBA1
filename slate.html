Model and assumptions (unchanged from lines.html)

RecordLine-1 is this project's own line-origination model. Its constants are documented assumptions, not official values (flag E-01). The engine-v0 total is additive ratings math with HCA deliberately not applied.

RecordLine-1: p_smoothed = (W+3)/(G+6) · margin = 35·(p_home − p_away) + 2.5 (HCA) · spread_home = −½round(margin) · p_win = 1/(1+e^(−margin/11)) · pick when |margin| ≥ 3.0
Engine v0: POSS = pace_h + pace_a − lg_pace · PTS = (OffRtg·DefRtg_opp/lg_OffRtg)·POSS/100 · TOTAL = PTS_h + PTS_a · SPREAD = PTS_h − PTS_a · HCA not applied (flagged)

RecordLine-1 constants are identical to lines.html (scripts/build_edge_site.py); the v0 total formula is identical to scripts/originate_lines.py. Dispersion is measured from official finals (margin sd 16.13, total sd 17.13, n=14 games). Sources: NBA Teams Advanced · NBA Teams Traditional · finals 11/11 · finals 11/12.
