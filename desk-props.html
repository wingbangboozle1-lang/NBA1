Published as an interval because that is all it is.

Jokić points O + Jokić rebounds O0.3720.4740.17640.00000.3724-0.127The Fréchet bounds are what is certain without a correlation estimate; the measured ρ on 4 logged games is printed next to them to show how far from settled it is.

The Fréchet bounds hold for any dependence structure, so they are the only defensible statement without a measured correlation. A book's payout sits between them; without a price we cannot say whether it does.
