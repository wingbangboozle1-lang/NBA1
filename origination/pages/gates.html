Validation gates

Seven conjoined gates. Failing any one blocks promotion regardless of score.

No strategy is funded. All 12 flagged items and every unvalidated number are published here. There is no market odds feed, so nothing on this site is a claim of real-world profit.

The gates

Definitions, not data: these are this project's rules, so no external source applies.
GateRequirementWhy it exists
G1 Sample floor≥ 25 betsBelow this, a record cannot be distinguished from noise at any plausible effect size.
G2 Positive returnROI > 0 at the baseline priceNon-negotiable.
G3 Significancepnull ≤ 0.05 against 5,000 Monte-Carlo no-skill bettorsThe luck test proper: the null takes the identical bet list with outcomes drawn at the baseline probability.
G4 StabilityROI > 0 in both halves of the windowStops one hot week carrying a strategy.
G5 No look-aheadMechanicalEvery forecast is fitted only on games strictly before the game being forecast.
G6 Baseline sanityBrier ≤ 0.25 and MAE better than naiveIf the line itself is junk, nothing measured against it means anything.
G7 Multiple comparisonspnull ≤ 0.01274Šidák correction across all 4 cells that reach the floor. Testing 39 cells at α = 0.05 would produce false positives by construction.
