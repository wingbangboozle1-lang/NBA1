HighQ5. MediumQ6. Is a 4-percentage-point edge the right trigger to trade?The full grid (2 / 3 / 4 points) is published. Get it wrong and a real signal is either diluted below noise or never traded at all.Fix the trigger by rule, not by result: choose the smallest trigger whose minimum detectable ROI at the current sample is below the plausible true edge. Revisit only when the sample triples.official source
MediumQ7. How should the leaderboard reward a strategy that is merely unproven?Four reward tiers are defined; every live cell is currently in Tier 0 or Tier 1. MediumQ12. Is the rest signal real?Rest-day differential regressed on out-of-sample margin error gives +2.82 points per day (t = 1.53, p = 0.130, n = 101). Four-day density gives +0.51 (p = 0.849).Whether any of those survive a proper sample.This is the only candidate signal in the whole study that is not obviously zero. It deserves to be the first thing re-tested when the sample grows.Pre-register the rest signal as the primary hypothesis and re-test it at 250 and 400 games. Do not add new signals until it resolves.official source
