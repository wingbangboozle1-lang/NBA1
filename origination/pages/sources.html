What is deliberately not used

  • Efficiency numbers from earlier sessions. They were captured from NBA.com Stats tables that render client-side and could not be re-read here, so they could not be re-verified this session. Nothing in this build depends on them.
  • Any odds source. None is official.
  • Any inferred result. The 23 games published without scores are listed as fixtures with blank scores, even though the standings residual would determine them.
