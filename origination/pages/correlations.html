What this means for parlays

  • Same-game parlays: the measured joint probability of the home side covering and the game going over is 0.2970 against an independence value of 0.2500 — a gap of +4.7 percentage points. On 101 games the standard error of that joint estimate is 0.0431, so the gap is 1.09 standard errors from independence: not significant (Fréchet bounds for two 0.50 events are [0.00, 0.50]). Price same-game parlays at independence until this resolves.
  • Cross-game parlays: the correlation between cover outcomes of different games on the same slate is r = -0.030 over 750 ordered pairs (p = 0.41). Independence across games is a defensible assumption here.
  • Margin and total are close to uncorrelated: r = -0.030 over 151 games. A blowout does not reliably change the scoring environment in this sample.
