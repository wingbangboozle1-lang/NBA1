How to read these numbers

  • Home spread is from the home team’s point of view. -6.5 means the home team is favoured by 6.5; +6.5 means it is getting 6.5 points. It is the negative of the model’s predicted home margin, so by construction the model thinks each side is a coin flip against it.
  • Total is the sum of the two teams’ opponent-adjusted points predictions.
  • ML is the fair American price implied by a normal distribution around the predicted margin with an out-of-sample standard deviation of 15.87 points. It carries no margin, because this desk is not a book.
  • Signal shift is what the rest and back-to-back strategies would add. It is shown beside the line and not inside it, because no strategy has passed a gate.
  • Rows tagged wide have a spread of 15 points or more. The ratings behind them rest on 10–12 games per team; see F6.
