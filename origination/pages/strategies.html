The library

Every strategy is the originated line plus one extra official input the line does not use. The line is the baseline; the strategy is the claim that something else matters. Each strategy is run at three trigger levels (the smallest probability edge it will bet on), and every cell is published — the trigger is not tuned.

What each strategy claims

S10@0.02 Adaptive Rest Model (spread)

Signal: Rest days and back-to-back flags, with the WEIGHTS fitted on past out-of-sample errors.

Instead of fixed 0.55 / 1.60 weights, this refits the rest and back-to-back coefficients every slate using only the forecast errors of games already played, then applies them forward. It answers the question the fixed-weight strategies cannot: is the signal real but mis-sized, or is it simply not there?

S1@0.02 Rest Edge (spread)

Signal: Rest days and back-to-back status from the official schedule.

Shifts the originated spread by 0.55 points per day of rest advantage plus 1.60 points against any team on the second night of a back-to-back. Bets the side when the shift is worth at least the trigger probability edge.

S6T@0.02 Short-Rest Overs (total)

Signal: Rest days from the official schedule.

Adds 0.30 points to the originated total for each day of rest a team is short of three, capped at three days. Bets the over when that is worth at least the trigger.

S2@0.02 Back-to-Back Fade (spread)

Signal: Back-to-back flag derived from consecutive official schedule dates.

Fades the team playing the second game in two nights by 1.60 points against the originated spread. Bets only when a back-to-back is present.

S6@0.02 Schedule Density (spread)

Signal: Games played in the previous four days, counted from consecutive official schedule dates.

Penalises a team by 0.90 points of spread for every extra game it has played in the previous four days relative to its opponent. A schedule-density signal the baseline rating ignores entirely.

S8@0.02 Cross-Game Parlay (two spreads)

Signal: Rest days and back-to-back status from the official schedule.

Takes the two largest rest-signal spread edges available on one slate and parlays them across two different games. Priced at independence; the correlation page tests whether that assumption holds.

S0@0.02 Null Control (random moneyline)

Signal: None - a uniform random probability.

Takes the same number of moneyline bets at random, with no signal at all. Any gate this control passes is a gate too weak to detect skill.

S4@0.02 Rest Advantage (moneyline)

Signal: Rest days and back-to-back status from the official schedule.

Re-prices the moneyline after the rest adjustment and bets when the new price is at least 4 percentage points better than the originated price.

S5@0.02 Rested Underdog (moneyline)

Signal: Rest days, back-to-back flag and the originated spread.

Backs the underdog by the originated spread only when that underdog also holds a rest advantage and the favourite is on the second night of a back-to-back.

S3@0.02 Fatigue Unders (total)

Signal: Back-to-back flag derived from consecutive official schedule dates.

Removes 1.70 points from the originated total for each team on the second night of a back-to-back. Bets the under when the shift is worth at least the trigger probability edge.

S7@0.02 Correlation SGP (spread + total)

Signal: Rest and back-to-back flags, priced with the measured margin/total correlation.

Combines the rest spread leg and the fatigue total leg in one same-game parlay. Pricing uses the bivariate normal with the correlation MEASURED on the out-of-sample window, not an assumed independence.

S11@0.02 Adaptive Fatigue Model (total)

Signal: Back-to-back count, weight fitted on past out-of-sample errors.

Same adaptive method applied to the totals market.

S9@0.02 Player-Prop Combos (points + rebounds + assists)

Signal: Would require a verified per-game player box-score log.

Combines two or three player props from the same game. NOT BUILT: originating a player-prop line requires each player's game-by-game box score, and no official page available in this environment publishes a full season of them. Only two partial official leaderboards exist - the top 50 scorers and the top 24 by minutes - and neither is a game log. Nothing is estimated in their place.

Blocked: No verified per-game player log. The official Players > Traditional table is rendered client-side and only page 1 (50 players, season averages) is reachable; a prop line needs the game-by-game distribution behind those averages.
