Ratings

Two separable models, both fitted on the 151 official final scores:

  • Rating — Simple Rating System: a team’s average margin adjusted for the ratings of its opponents, shrunk toward zero with 4 pseudo-games. Drives the spread and the moneyline.
  • Offence / defence — opponent-adjusted points scored and points allowed, shrunk toward the league mean with 6 pseudo-games. Drives the total.

These are points-per-game scale, not NBA.com’s per-100-possession efficiency: possessions are not published on any page reachable here (F5).
