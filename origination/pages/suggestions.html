No strategy is funded. All 12 flagged items and every unvalidated number are published here. There is no market odds feed, so nothing on this site is a claim of real-world profit.

Suggestions, ranked — none implemented

This page exists so that a change is chosen before it is made. Nothing here has been built: the whole point is that these are options for someone to review and pick between. Each row states what it unlocks, what it costs, what could go wrong, and what the recommendation would be.
