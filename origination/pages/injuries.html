Rotation impact

The question the brief asks — how an absence changes the rotation and therefore the production — needs two things this project does not have: the absent player’s minutes and usage, and the replacement’s. Neither is published in game-by-game form anywhere reachable here. So:

  • Where a listed player appears in one of the official leaderboards, their verified season minutes and points are shown in the player-reference table below.
  • Where they do not, the cells are blank and flagged.
  • No injury adjustment is applied to any originated line. Every spread and total on this site is computed without it, and is labelled as such.
