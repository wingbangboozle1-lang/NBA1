What game-flow analysis cannot say here

Genuine game-flow work — how a team performs when leading or trailing, how a run changes pace, how a coach’s rotation responds to foul trouble — needs play-by-play or at least quarter-by-quarter data. Neither is published in the pages reachable here. What is above is the honest subset: schedule-derived fatigue, and scoring and margin profiles from final scores.
