Originated lines & picks

Our own lines for every game of the Nov 13–17, 2026 window (refreshed 2026-11-14; slates through 11/17 = 3 days ahead), generated by model RecordLine-1 from official NBA records. These are our numbers — not sportsbook prices. Every pick is a paper record gated behind the validation framework.

Read this first. Model parameters below are documented assumptions, not official values. No market odds feed is connected, so market-comparison gates (G4 closing-line value, G5 price edge) are blocked — nothing here is a validated bet or betting advice. Player totals (over/under team totals) are not originated: pace/efficiency inputs are missing. See Flagged for review.

Model RecordLine-1 — formula and assumptions

  • Smoothed win probability: p = (W + 3) / (G + 6) — a +3-win / +6-game shrink toward .500 (assumption; early-season records are noisy).
  • Expected home margin: 35.0 × (p_home − p_away) + 2.5 — scale K = 35.0 points per 1.000 pct difference and home-court +2.5 pts (assumptions, not official values).
  • Win probability: P(home) = 1 / (1 + e^(−margin / 11.0)) with σ = 11.0 (assumption).
  • Originated spread = −expected margin rounded to 0.5. Fair ML decimal odds = 1/p (no margin baked in).
  • Pick gate G-CFG: pick only when |expected margin| ≥ 3.0; otherwise PASS (no bet).
  • Inputs: official records from NBA.com slate pages (cross-checked on official game pages). Reproducible audit: data/originated_lines.json.
