A newer official injury edition exists than the one the model uses

The lines on this site are built on the 11/13/26 07:30 PM ET edition. On 2026-11-14 this session read a newer one, the 11/14/26 04:15 AM ET edition (official PDF), and transcribed all 49 rows to verify/2026-11-14/official_injury_2026-11-14_0415AM_s934ca56f.json. It has deliberately not been fed into the model, because it would move every 11/14 line and that is a reviewer decision, not an integration step.

What it changes in practice: very little. The same five of the ten teams playing on 11/14 (New Orleans Pelicans, Cleveland Cavaliers, Memphis Grizzlies, Chicago Bulls, Miami Heat) still print NOT YET SUBMITTED; the teams that filed are Denver Nuggets, Dallas Mavericks, Brooklyn Nets, Minnesota Timberwolves, Detroit Pistons. The 26 player-level rows are Denver, Dallas, Brooklyn, Minnesota and Detroit. The 11/15 games do appear in this edition but every team is NOT YET SUBMITTED, and no 11/16 or 11/17 game appears at all. So the older edition was not hiding a status change — it was simply one publication cycle behind.
