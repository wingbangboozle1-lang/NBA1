Decisions: pick a path

Six decisions in dependency order, the 19 open questions mapped to the decision that closes each one, and the 18 suggestions ranked for review. Nothing here is implemented unless it says so.

What this page is for

Six decisions, in the order they unblock each other. Each one states what is actually known, the options with their real cost, which open questions it answers, which suggestions it consumes, and what a reviewer has to decide before any of it can be built. Nothing on this page has been implemented except where a row says so.

The evidence behind every claim is on the pages linked in each row. The open questions and the suggestion catalogue are unchanged and stay published in full on Open Questions and Suggestions; this page is the reading order, not a replacement.

Pick a path

In dependency order. P1 is cheap and unblocks nothing; P2 unblocks the leaderboard; P3 is the only one that needs money; P4 and P5 are the two that change what the site publishes; P6 is the one that makes the rest stay true.

P1 — Decide whether the hour of the capture is part of the product

Why it is a decision: The 18:40Z re-read found the site pricing from a 04:15 AM ET injury edition while the 01:30 PM ET edition was published and four of the five teams it called unfiled had filed. Nothing about the model is wrong; the site simply had no rule for how old a capture may be before it must be labelled or refreshed. This is the cheapest decision here and it is the one that keeps every other number honest.

Q20 Can a prop edge ever be big enough to bet, given the noise? Measured dispersion puts the answer in reach: on 2026-11-14 the largest gap between our projection and the player's own season average is 0.52 of a measured SD, and the median is 0.16 SD. With a points CV of 0.344, a prop needs roughly a 7-9 point projection edge to be one SD away from the anchor - larger than any factor this model applies. What is missing is the thing no official source publishes: the line a market would actually hang. Without it, the question 'is our number better than theirs' cannot be asked of props any more than of spreads (Q1). Either (a) log real prop lines and closes in data/raw/market.json with per-row sources and compare, or (b) accept the desk rule this page prints - publish a prop only when our projection is at least one measured SD from the naive anchor - and let the count of publishable props stay near zero while the model is what it is. Q6 Does the Emirates NBA Cup change how teams play? Any measurement. Twenty-five of the 51 games in the test window were Cup group games, and point differential is the Cup tiebreaker, which plausibly changes late-game behaviour in blowouts - but nothing in the captured data measures it.
