Parlay pricing

Measured rho, with the naive product printed beside it instead of a single withheld price.

Correction 2026-11-16 (F107, ledger entry 10). The first publication of this page priced every parlay and same-game parlay three times too high: the joint-probability routine used Simpson weights without the h/3 factor, so joint probabilities above 1.000 were published (ORL@BOS / MIL@MIA was printed as 1.6171 against a naive product of 0.5375). The factor has been restored, the routine was checked against two closed forms, and every row below is recomputed and re-published, still before the tip of the earliest game it covers. No single-leg ticket was affected. Ledger entries 6 and 8 are superseded by entry 9.
Every parlay is priced with a measured rho and the naive independent product is printed immediately beside it, so the reader can see exactly what the correlation assumption is worth.
