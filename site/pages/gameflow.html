Expected margin is from the home team's point of view: positive means the home team is favoured. P(margin ≤5) is the probability the game is decided by five points or fewer; P(margin ≥20) is the probability of a twenty-point blow-out. P(OT) is the measured season overtime rate: 8 of 160 completed games went to overtime (rate 0.05, window 2026-10-20..2026-11-13) — priced from the measurement instead of the model's per-game approximation (0.005–0.023 on this slate), which the season shows is materially miscalibrated (realised 0.050 vs ~0.019 mean, exact test p = 0.012; see the strategy-lab probability audit and flag F64). The un-calibrated per-game approximation is kept in docs/data/gameflow.json as p_overtime_approx for research. Margin and total probabilities come from official pace and efficiency inputs plus the fitted outcome spread — no play-by-play input exists, so no run, timeout or quarter-split metric is published.

Nothing on this site is a betting recommendation, and no market price is used anywhere: every "edge" is against a naive model that stands in for the market, because no official NBA-operated price feed exists.
