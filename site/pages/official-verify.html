What this page does not check

Stated so the page cannot be read as a clean bill of health.

  • It does not check any derived number. A spread, a total, a moneyline, a win probability, an ROI, a p-value or a gate result is model output, and no official page publishes a value to compare it against. Those numbers are checked on the Validation and Backtest pages, and the ones that can be recomputed from this repository's own rows are recomputed there.
  • It does not check that a page stays available. A 200 at read time is the only claim.
  • It does not settle anything. Settlement against the 11/13 finals is on the Lines & Picks page and in the settlement record.
  • It reads the pages once. Two readers an hour apart can disagree, and the injury-report columns are exactly where that happens.
