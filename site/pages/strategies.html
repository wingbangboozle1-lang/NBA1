The strategy library

What each strategy actually is, and why it was written.

ID Name Family Market Rule Threshold Why Ungraded reason Source
S1 Side edge vs reference Single bet Point spread Play the side when the originated margin differs from the reference spread by at least 3.0 points. 3.0 pts The additive efficiency model collapses to a net-rating difference, so a side edge can only come from shrinkage, home court, rest and availability. A 3.0-point filter keeps only the plays where those refinements actually bite. — S1
S2 Total edge vs reference Single bet Game total Play over/under when the originated total differs from the reference total by at least 6.0 points. 6.0 pts The reference total is simply the two teams' published scoring averages added together, so it ignores defence entirely. The gap is the opponent-adjustment signal. — S1
S3 Moneyline value Single bet Moneyline Play the moneyline when the originated win probability differs from the reference win probability by at least 6 percentage points and the originated price is shorter than +200. 6 pp Moneylines punish favourites less than spreads do, so a large probability gap is the efficient way to monetise a side edge on a big favourite. — S1
S4 Pace-driven total Single bet Game total Totals only, and only when the game's expected pace is at least 3.0 possessions per 48 minutes away from the league average AND the total edge is at least 4.0 points. pace >= 3.0 and 4.0 pts Pace is the most persistent team stat in the official advanced table. Restricting totals to genuine pace outliers removes the games where the total edge is just defensive noise. — S1
S5 Back-to-back fade Single bet Point spread Exactly one team is on the second night of a back-to-back and the originated margin differs from the reference spread by at least 2.0 points. 2.0 pts Rest is the one schedule effect that is fully observable from the official schedule and results. Both teams are held to the same threshold so the filter is not simply 'fade the tired team'. — S1
S6 Availability shock Single bet Point spread / total At least 15% of one team's minutes are at risk in the official injury report and the originated number differs from the reference by at least 2.0 points. 15% minutes and 2.0 pts Minutes at risk is measurable from the official injury report plus the official minutes table. UNGRADED: no historical injury-report editions were captured, so this strategy cannot be scored yet. Requires archived official injury reports (Flagged F5). S1
S7 Player points prop Player prop Player points Play over/under a player's season scoring average when the originated projection differs from that average by at least 2.0 points. 2.0 pts Season average is the reference prop line; possession, opponent-defence and teammate-availability multipliers are the signal. UNGRADED: no player game logs were captured, so projection accuracy is unmeasured. Requires player game logs to measure game-to-game variance (Flagged F2/Q3). S1
S8 Points + rebounds + assists combo Player prop Player PRA Play over/under a player's season PRA average when the originated PRA projection differs by at least 3.0. 3.0 PRA Combining three categories averages out single-category noise. UNGRADED for the same reason as S7. Requires player game logs to measure game-to-game variance (Flagged F2/Q3). S1
S9 Two-leg cross-game parlay Parlay Parlay Take the two highest-edge S1/S2 legs of the day from DIFFERENT games, each with an edge at least 2.5, and parlay them. 2.5 per leg Legs from different games are independent, so the parlay price is the product of the legs and the only edge is the model edge on each leg. — S1
S10 Same-game parlay (side + total) Parlay / SGP Same-game parlay In the same game, parlay the side leg and the total leg when each has an edge of at least 2.5 and the modelled side/total correlation is at most 0.30. 2.5 per leg, rho <= 0.30 Same-game legs share possessions, so they are correlated. Pricing them as independent is the classic SGP error; the copula price is published next to the independent price so the difference is visible. — S1
