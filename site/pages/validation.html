The seven gates

Every strategy must clear all seven. A gate that has not been run is recorded as NOT RUN, never as a pass.

ID Gate Definition Source
G1 Sample size At least 30 graded plays; 10-29 is provisional. S1
G2 Calibration Observed hit rate within 8 pp of the mean predicted probability. S1
G3 Edge over break-even Mean modelled probability at or above 52.38% (break-even at -110). S1
G4 Skill, not luck Wilson 95% lower bound on the hit rate above 52.38%; exact binomial p reported. S1
G5 Stability Bootstrap 95% CI of the model's margin MAE sits entirely below the reference model's MAE. S1
G6 Correlation limit Parlay leg pairs limited to |rho| <= 0.30. S1
G7 Availability robustness A play must survive flipping every Questionable player to Out (needs the official report for the graded window). S1

NBA1 — a research workspace for originating NBA lines and testing whether the signal is skill or luck. Nothing on this site is a betting recommendation, and no market price is used anywhere: every "edge" is against a naive model that stands in for the market, because no official NBA-operated price feed exists.
