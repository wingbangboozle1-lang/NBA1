The cheapest trustworthy signal in the system
Rest is derived from official game DATES only: either two official dates are one day apart or they are not. No score, no rating and no estimate is involved. That is why this layer is published even while the model layers are still provisional.

Two dates have no rows in the official log, so some rest figures are lower bounds
The official game log prints 2026-10-24 and 2026-11-02 with no rows at all (flag F1) and holds 23 fixtures with no score. Those two dates are missing from every rest figure, so a rest value that crosses one of them is measured from the previous dated game and is therefore a LOWER bound on days off. The row-level note says this; nothing is filled in.
