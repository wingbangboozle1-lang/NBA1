Estimator disagreement — the headline signal

Two independent estimators of every game (A: possession × points-per-possession; B: points-scored/points-allowed with opponent and pace adjustment) are built from the official team tables. Their disagreement |P(A) − P(B)| in percentage points is the primary confidence signal: small means the two constructions agree, large means the line is not safe to publish. The blended line is published underneath, never on top.

How a pick becomes a pick

Each strategy is a rule over the originated lines. A play is only published when the strategy's own rule fires; a strategy that has not cleared the sample and skill gates is capped at a quarter unit or held back as research-only, and that cap is shown on the leaderboard. No pick here is market-validated, because no official price feed exists; a win would mean the model beat a naive model, not that it beat a book.
