How a pick becomes a pick

Each strategy is a rule over the originated lines. A play is only published when the strategy's own rule fires; a strategy that has not cleared the sample and skill gates is capped at a quarter unit or held back as research-only, and that cap is shown on the leaderboard. No pick here is market-validated, because no official price feed exists; a win would mean the model beat a naive model, not that it beat a book.

NBA1 — a research workspace for originating NBA lines and testing whether the signal is skill or luck. Nothing on this site is a betting recommendation, and no market price is used anywhere: every "edge" is against a naive model that stands in for the market, because no official NBA-operated price feed exists.
