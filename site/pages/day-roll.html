How to read 3W-10L

This is one slate, 13 gradable picks, all of them graded against our own number. It is not evidence of skill or of its absence: the win count against a self-priced line has no vig, no closing line and no market, and a single slate is far inside the noise band. The graded window that the leaderboard uses is on Out-of-Sample Backtest; the point of this table is that a losing slate is published as a losing slate (-5.80 units at the -110 convention), with the settlement stamp attached to each row rather than a summary number elsewhere.
