Honest headline: The model wins on margin (MAE 12.98 vs 13.62) and loses on totals (14.83 vs 14.72) and on straight-up winners (40-20 vs 42-18). The reference has the better Brier score (0.2001 vs 0.2020) and the better log loss (0.5827 vs 0.5873). Each added game to the window has moved at least one claim — treat single-window results as hypotheses, not findings.

Source: official NBA.com game scores for all 60 games. Team ratings computed only from games before each predicted game (no look-ahead). Full game-by-game detail: data/derived/backtest.json.
