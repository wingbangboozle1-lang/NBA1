How a game day gets settled

  1. The scheduled job runs scripts/settle_slate.py for every archived pre-tip slate.
  2. It refuses any date where even one game is not verified final (exit 3).
  3. It grades only the archived pre-tip picks, and asserts their SHA-256 against the ledger entry that committed to them before the first tip.
  4. It appends two ledger entries: a settlement-verification entry and the settlement record, then re-verifies the whole chain.
  5. The settlement stamp above is read from that record, so the page cannot show a stamp the ledger does not hold.
