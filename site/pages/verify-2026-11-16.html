A team moving from NOT YET SUBMITTED to filed is the most consequential kind of change and the one a player-keyed diff misses entirely: there is no player row on either side to compare. It is tracked separately for exactly that reason.

5 teams had still not filed at 11/15/2026 09:00 PM: washington wizards (MEM@WAS), boston celtics (ORL@BOS), houston rockets (HOU@MIA), philadelphia 76ers (PHI@BKN), phoenix suns (PHX@DEN). Their rows are published as NOT YET SUBMITTED, never as “healthy”.
A defect in the repository’s own stored edition.
A NOT YET SUBMITTED row is a team-level marker and the league prints no player on it. These stored rows carry a player name inherited from the preceding row, and one of them is encoded status='Out' — so any consumer reading the status column alone counts a player who was never named as ruled out for a team that had filed nothing. Published as flag F101 rather than silently corrected, because the row is also the record that the team had not filed.

6. What this pass could not do

The original PDF bytes could not be archived. The brief asks for every injury PDF to be stored with its timestamp. This environment has no outbound network — curl to ak-static.cms.nba.com fails at the TLS handshake (OpenSSL SSL_connect: SSL_ERROR_SYSCALL, HTTP 000, 0 bytes) — and the agent-side fetcher returns parsed text only. So no SHA-256 of the PDF and no byte-identical copy exist. What is archived is the edition stamp the PDF prints, the exact URL, the UTC retrieval time, the printed row count and the full row transcription. Published as flag F100.
The 2026-11-15 slate is not settled. At 21:2x ET on 11/15, six of its nine games were final and three were still in progress. The settlement rule requires the official page to print Final with quarter linescores summing to it, so the slate stays open rather than being settled from live scores. Published as flag F99.
