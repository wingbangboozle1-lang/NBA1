Noise-band rule. A team with |z| ≥ 1.96 is outside the noise band — its record is statistically distinguishable from luck at the 5% level. A strategy that leans on such a team (credits its record to skill) is refused publication until the edge is explained by a repeatable signal.

Method: Pythagorean expectation (exponent 13.91) on official OffRtg/DefRtg. Expected wins = GP × pythagorean win%. Luck = actual wins - expected wins. Z divides luck by the binomial SD over games played. Source: NBA.com Team Advanced.
