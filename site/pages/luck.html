Expected wins come from Pythagorean expectation on the team's official net rating; luck wins are actual wins minus expected wins; z is luck wins divided by the standard deviation of a binomial with that many games. z above +1.5 is a team that has been lucky; below -1.5, unlucky. A team with a large positive luck figure is a candidate to fade, not to follow.

Why this page exists
Luck is the thing that has to be removed before a record can be read as skill. A strategy that went 23-9 might have been right 23 times, or might have been right 17 times and been lucky 6. The gate that tests this is G4, and S1 (23-9) is the first strategy to clear it (exact binomial p = 0.020) - which is why it is finally allowed to be called something other than luck. It is still capped by the calibration and stability gates it did not clear.
