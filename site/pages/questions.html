A4 How much of each team's record is luck rather than skill? See the Luck page. Pythagorean expectation from the official OffRtg and DefRtg gives each team an expected win total; the difference from actual wins, divided by the binomial standard deviation over games played, is the luck z-score. With 10-13 games played, roughly two thirds of the league sits inside one standard deviation, which is what you would expect this early.
