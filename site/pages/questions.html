Q20 Can a prop edge ever be big enough to bet, given the noise? Measured dispersion puts the answer in reach: on 2026-11-14 the largest gap between our projection and the player's own season average is 0.52 of a measured SD, and the median is 0.16 SD. With a points CV of 0.344, a prop needs roughly a 7-9 point projection edge to be one SD away from the anchor - larger than any factor this model applies. What is missing is the thing no official source publishes: the line a market would actually hang. Without it, the question 'is our number better than theirs' cannot be asked of props any more than of spreads (Q1). Either (a) log real prop lines and closes in data/raw/market.json with per-row sources and compare, or (b) accept the desk rule this page prints - publish a prop only when our projection is at least one measured SD from the naive anchor - and let the count of publishable props stay near zero while the model is what it is.
