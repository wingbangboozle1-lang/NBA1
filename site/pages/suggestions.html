SG1 Data Add a results backfill covering the whole season The out-of-sample window is 28 games because only 2026-11-08 onward was captured. The season began 2026-10-20, so roughly 200 games are missing and no strategy can reach the 30-play sample gate. - Backfill job that walks nba.com/games one date at a time from 2026-10-20 (effort: Low - one script, one-off run; risk: Low - additive, existing files untouched; unlocks: Q2, F8, F9; every gate result becomes meaningful)
- Leave the window at 28 games and let it grow naturally (effort: None; risk: High - roughly two more months before any strategy clears the sample gate; unlocks: Nothing new)		 Take option 1. It is the cheapest change on this list and it is a prerequisite for four other items. Approve the backfill run and confirm the retention policy for historical snapshots. S14
SG2 Data Log real closing prices Every 'edge' on the site is edge against a naive model standing in for the market (F3). Without real prices there is no way to say whether any of this is profitable. - Add data/raw/market.json populated from a licensed odds source, with a source field per row (effort: Medium; risk: Medium - introduces a non-NBA source, so the per-row source link becomes mandatory; unlocks: Q1, Q8; real ROI; closing-line-value tracking)
- Manual closing-line log in a CSV committed to the repo (effort: Low per day, high over a season; risk: Medium - human transcription error; unlocks: The same thing, more slowly)
- Do nothing and keep the model-versus-reference framing (effort: None; risk: High - the leaderboard can never claim profitability; unlocks: Nothing)		 Option 1 if a licensed feed is available, option 2 as a stopgap. Until either happens, the site should keep saying 'model-vs-reference units' rather than 'profit'. Whether to introduce a non-NBA data source at all, and which one. S15
SG3 Data Complete the player-minutes table Minutes are known for 50 of 465 players (F2). The availability impact is therefore a floor and is 0.00 for several teams that are clearly missing rotation players. - Fetch the official players table filtered by team, 30 requests, and union the results (effort: Medium; risk: Low - same official source; unlocks: Q4, a trustworthy availability column, grading S6)
- Derive minutes from official box scores as they are captured (effort: Medium; risk: Low; unlocks: Q3 and Q4 together)
- Keep the top-50 floor and publish the coverage column (effort: None; risk: Medium - a 0.00 can be misread as 'no impact'; unlocks: Nothing)		 Option 1 or 2. If neither is taken, the Injuries page must keep the coverage column and confidence label visible so a 0.00 is never read as healthy. Which capture path to fund. S7
SG4 Site / UI Show the confidence label next to every derived number The site mixes three kinds of number - official values, arithmetic on official values, and modelling choices. They look identical in a table. - Three-colour provenance chip on every cell: official, derived, assumption (effort: Medium; risk: Low; unlocks: Trust; fewer misread tables)
- Keep provenance in a footnote column (effort: Low; risk: Medium - easy to miss; unlocks: Partial)		 Option 1. The whole verification rule depends on the reader being able to tell these apart at a glance. Visual treatment and whether the chip applies to every table or only the derived ones. S1
SG5 Site / UI Add a graded-results page that updates itself Picks are published as PENDING and nothing currently grades them once games finish. - Extend the refresh job to re-grade every pending pick against the official final score and recompute the leaderboard (effort: Medium; risk: Low; unlocks: A living leaderboard; the reward tiers start to mean something)
- Grade manually at the end of each week (effort: High over a season; risk: High - drift between what was published and what was graded; unlocks: Partial)		 Option 1, and it should be automatic - grading must not depend on anyone remembering. Whether graded picks overwrite the published slate or are kept alongside it. S14
SG6 Site / UI Separate the two meanings of 'edge' everywhere in the UI 'Edge' currently means the gap between the originated number and the reference line. A reader will assume it means edge against a book. - Rename to 'model gap vs reference' throughout, with a tooltip (effort: Low; risk: Low; unlocks: Honesty at no cost)
- Keep 'edge' and add a banner (effort: Low; risk: Medium - banners get ignored; unlocks: Partial)		 Option 1, and reserve the word 'edge' for a market comparison once SG2 lands. Wording only. S15
SG7 Model Make the gates do something Gates are currently reported but not enforced: a strategy that fails G1 still appears on the Picks page. - Tier-driven staking - the leaderboard tier sets the published unit size, and anything below Tier 3 is published as paper only (effort: Medium; risk: Low; unlocks: The leaderboard becomes an actual allocation rule)
- Hard suppression - hide plays from strategies that fail a gate (effort: Low; risk: Medium - hides information the reader may want; unlocks: Cleaner pick lists, less transparency)
- Report only (effort: None; risk: Medium - invites reading research plays as recommendations; unlocks: Nothing)		 Option 1. Tier-driven staking keeps the information visible while making the status unambiguous. Unit sizes per tier, and whether paper-only plays are visually separated. S2
SG8 Model Handle overtime and extreme games explicitly A 284-point double-overtime game sits in a 28-game sample and inflates the fitted total-error spread, which in turn flattens every probability the model produces. - Model regulation separately and add an overtime term (effort: High; risk: Medium; unlocks: Q7, better total calibration)
- Winsorise the residuals when fitting the spread (effort: Low; risk: Medium - hides genuine tail risk; unlocks: More stable parameters now)
- Leave it and grow the sample (effort: None; risk: Medium; unlocks: Nothing)		 Option 2 as a stopgap alongside SG1; revisit option 1 once the sample is large enough to evaluate it. Whether tail risk should be damped or modelled. S3_2026-11-12
SG9 Model Add an Emirates NBA Cup adjustment - but only after measuring it The 2026-11-13 slate is entirely Cup group play.
