Reading rule. Every table row either links to the official source it came from or carries an explicit no-source mark with a stated reason (the mark explains why no official source exists or could confirm the cell). A blank or dashed cell means the value could not be confirmed from an official source — it is never filled in by guesswork. See Flagged for Review.
