Irregularities found

Things a reviewer should look at. None of them is a fabricated value; each one is a documented gap or a conflict between two official readings.

ID Severity Irregularity Detail Resolution Source
IR-01 High Two transcriptions of the same official PDF disagree on one player's team Build A's transcription of the 11/13/26 05:15 PM injury report records 'Martin, Caleb' under Cleveland Cavaliers with a blank matchup. In the PDF the row sits inside the Dallas Mavericks block, between 'Lively II, Dereck' and the Cleveland Cavaliers 'NOT YET SUBMITTED' row, and the official Dallas roster lists Caleb Martin (1628997, traded from PHI on 02/04/25). Cleveland's official roster does not list him. This build records him as Dallas Mavericks, on the PDF's block structure and corroborated by the official roster page. Build A's page still shows the earlier attribution; the conflict is published rather than silently corrected. link
IR-02 High No final score exists for the 2026-11-13 slate At capture time (2026-11-14T01:20Z, 2026-11-13 21:20 ET) the 11/13 games were in progress: the official Games & Scores page shows NYK 35 - IND 40 at Q2 6:55 and MIL 30 - ORL 36 at Q2 4:48, with the remaining seven games marked PREGAME. No final score for any 11/13 game is published anywhere in the captures. Left open. The 9 games are carried in data/raw/prior_slate.json for rest and back-to-back flags only, with no score and no inferred result. The ratings window therefore still ends at 2026-11-12. link
IR-03 Medium 18 team records are one night out of date Records are read from the official slate pages at capture time, before the 11/13 games finish. The 18 teams that played on 11/13 (NYK, IND, MIL, ORL, CLE, MIA, ATL, CHI, MEM, NOP, UTA, HOU, GSW, SAS, POR, SAC, OKC, LAC) will each have a changed record once that slate settles. Left open and published. The next capture of the slate pages resolves it automatically. link
IR-04 High The 11/14 injury report covers only half the teams At the 07:30 PM ET edition, five of the ten teams playing on 11/14 had not filed: New Orleans, Cleveland, Memphis, Chicago and Miami are printed as NOT YET SUBMITTED. Only Denver, Dallas, Brooklyn, Minnesota and Detroit filed. Published as NOT YET SUBMITTED with the official PDF link. The availability impact for those five teams is unknown and is shown as such; it is never filled in. link
IR-05 Medium No injury-report edition yet covers the 11/15 to 11/17 games The 07:30 PM ET edition of the 11/13 report prints rows for the 11/13 and 11/14 games only. The official injury-report policy requires a report for each day's games, so editions for 11/15, 11/16 and 11/17 will be published later and do not exist at capture time. Those teams show as 'NO ENTRY IN THE 11/13/26 07:30 PM ET EDITION'. The lines for those games carry no injury adjustment, and that is stated on the page. link
IR-06 Low The 10:30 PM ET edition of the 11/13 report returns AccessDenied The league publishes a report at 10:30 PM ET for late games. The path for that edition returns an AccessDenied error rather than a document, so it was not published at capture time (21:20 ET). The 07:30 PM ET edition is used as the newest available. The next capture will pick up the later edition automatically. link
IR-07 High Player minutes are known for 50 of 465 players The official Players Traditional table publishes 465 rows across ten pages and only page 1 - the 50 highest scorers - is present in the page source a plain fetch can read. Minutes for the other 415 players are unknown. Blank, not estimated. The availability impact column is therefore a measured lower bound, and the minutes-coverage column is printed next to every injury row so the reader can see how much of the rotation is actually known. link
IR-08 High No official price exists, so 'edge' is measured against a model No NBA-operated source publishes a sportsbook price. Every edge on this site is therefore the originated model against a deliberately naive reference model built from the same official ratings - not an edge against a book. Stated on the lines, picks, strategies and validation pages, and carried as flag F3. No pick is described as market-validated. link
IR-09 Low Two conventions for a blank Team cell existed across builds Build A left every row whose Team cell was blank in the PDF unattributed and flagged it. Build B repeated the team of the block. Both are defensible readings of the same document, and they produce different answers for the same player. This build adopts one rule, states it on the page, records the basis row by row in data/d/injury_0730PM_team_basis.json, and corroborates the two page-break cases against official roster pages. IR-01 is the one place the two readings actually conflict. link
IR-10 Info Three pull requests from earlier sessions remain open PR #11 (arena/c7fbc064-nba1), PR #14 (arena/7cfd203a-nba1) and PR #20 (arena/9abdf3f7-nba1) are still open and belong to other branches. NBA1 — a research workspace for originating NBA lines and testing whether the signal is skill or luck. Nothing on this site is a betting recommendation, and no market price is used anywhere: every "edge" is against a naive model that stands in for the market, because no official NBA-operated price feed exists.
