Player game logs

Minutes, points, rebounds and assists for every player in the official gamebook of each finished game — not just the players on the injury report, because a rotation question needs the players who actually played. The source is the league's own gamebook PDF; the text of every page used is committed under data/official/gamebooks/ so each cell can be checked by eye.

The checks the parser must pass

Every team's parsed player points must equal the official final score. Minutes are compared with the gamebook's own printed team total. Result: 13 of 18 team totals match exactly; the largest difference is 3 second(s). Those differences are the official document's own (its printed total against the sum of its per-player MM:SS cells) and are published, not smoothed away.
