Decision register — 2026-11-19

What must be answered before anything is changed, what information this repository does not have, and the suggestions that would produce it.

Read this before choosing a path

The published picks lose, and they lose hardest where the model is most confident. Over the 37 decided paper singles: 14-23-1, hit rate 0.378 against a 0.524 break-even, -11.30 units. The model's mean published win probability on those same tickets was 0.720, so it was 34.1 points overconfident. The most confident bucket (published probability 0.70+) went 7-11 while claiming a mean probability of 0.795.

Why: Measured, not guessed: the model's probability divides its spread by the standard deviation of game margins (16.71 points), when the quantity that belongs in that division is the standard deviation of the error in its own spread. On the games already settled that error has sd 16.09 and a mean bias of +8.72 points — the model over-predicts the picked team's winning margin by 8.7 points on average. Because the pick gate keeps only |spread| >= 3.0, it keeps exactly the games where that bias is largest.

What it does not mean: It does not prove the model has no information. Sign accuracy was 14 of 37 at the model's own number, and the smallest bucket (3.0-5.0) actually went 4-3. It proves the model's CONFIDENCE is miscalibrated and its selection rule amplifies the error. Those are fixable; the fix is measurable; but until it is fixed no strategy on this site can be called skill.

D02 Should the probability be divided by the error in the line rather than by the spread of game margins? How to use this register

I01-I05 are ordered by the size of the error they remove, not by the effort they take — I02 removes a bias that affects every number on the site, and it is not the cheapest.

NBA1 — a research workspace for originating NBA lines and testing whether the signal is skill or luck. Nothing on this site is a betting recommendation, and no market price is used anywhere: every "edge" is against a naive model that stands in for the market, because no official NBA-operated price feed exists.
