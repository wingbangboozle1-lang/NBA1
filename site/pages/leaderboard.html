Leaderboard and rewards

The competition scoreboard: every strategy ranked, what it has earned, the gate blocking it, and what clearing that gate would be worth.

How to read this leaderboard

This is a competition with rewards, and this page is the scoreboard: ten strategies, ranked, each with what it would pay, what it has actually done on games played after the ratings cut-off, and the single gate that is stopping it from being promoted. Nothing here is funded. The reward for clearing every gate is a stake tier; the reward for not clearing them is a cap at a quarter unit or research-only status. That is the competition working, not failing.

The honest state: no strategy has cleared all seven gates. The best, S1, is 16-7 with an exact binomial p of 0.093 against a coin flip — good enough to keep watching, not good enough to call skill. Every figure below is read from docs/data/leaderboard.json and docs/data/validation.json.

G4 (skill, not luck) is the gate that separates this from a hot streak, and it is the one that fails for every strategy that has plays. It requires the Wilson 95% lower bound on the hit rate to clear the 52.38% break-even at -110. On 23 plays, 16 wins is consistent with luck (p = 0.093), so S1 stays capped no matter how good the record looks.
