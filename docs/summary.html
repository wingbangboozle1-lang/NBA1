Executive Summary

Important details across the NBA1 system: data, lines, strategies, picks, injuries/flow, verification, and open risks.

What was built

  • A self-contained engine (src/nba1) that originates lines, runs validated strategies, generates picks, and tracks injuries/game-flow — no manual input.
  • A live GitHub Pages dashboard with category subpages; every data row links to its official source.
  • Verified current-season (2026-27) data captured from Basketball-Reference and NBA.com.

Key figures

  • Upcoming slate: 26 games across 2026-11-13..16 (verified from NBA.com).
  • Generated picks: 34 (22 single-bet, 11 parlay, 1 defined player-prop combo).
  • Strategies: 6 across single / parlay / player-prop, each with 4 validation gates.
  • Correlations: SRS↔W/L% = 0.882, PA/G↔W/L% = -0.75.

Skill vs luck

Hit rates are Bayesian-shrunk toward 0.5 (prior 12) and a z-score significance gate (95%) decides skill vs luck. The 2026-11-12 demo slate (n=4) shrinks a 4/4 start to 0.625 and a 0/4 start to 0.375 — small samples are deliberately not over-credited.

Validation gates

G1 edge (≥5%) · G2 sample (≥30) · G3 correlation (|r|≤0.70) · G4 skill significance. A strategy is ranked only when all four pass. No strategy is fully ranked yet — the results feed is pending.

Risks & flags

  • Spread edge is structurally near-zero against our own fair line; real spread edges need a verified market line (flagged).
  • Live injury statuses unverified — rotation/production inputs are blank + flagged.
  • Player props are methodology-only until per-player stats + roster are verified.
  • 5 of 8 games on 2026-11-16 not captured in detail — needs a live re-pull.
