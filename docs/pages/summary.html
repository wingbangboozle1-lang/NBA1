Executive summary

The whole project on one page: what was built, what it found, what it cannot yet claim, and what would change the answer. Read this instead of the other thirteen pages if you only have two minutes.

The finding in one sentence

Lines can be originated from official data, but no strategy has demonstrated skill. The model produces all 31 game lines and 92 prop lines, and its totals are plausible. Zero of eight betting strategies pass the validation gates, because the largest graded sample available is 14 games against a floor of 100, those 14 games are look-ahead contaminated, and no real betting price was reachable to measure an edge against.

What is solid

  • The data is verified. Every dataset row carries an official NBA source link. Fifteen structural checks pass with zero errors: provenance hosts, schedule W-L reconciliation, pace against possessions, ratings against scoring, the scoring identity, played-game integrity, minutes monotonicity and absence-class consistency.
  • Dispersion is measured, not assumed. Total standard deviation 17.1 and margin standard deviation 16.1 points, taken from real finals. An earlier version guessed these and produced 30-point spreads and 99.9% win probabilities.
  • Ten team-level correlations are measured with p-values, led by pace against game total (r=0.792) and eFG% against offensive rating (r=0.819).
  • The injury logic avoids the classic double-count. Season ratings already exclude games a player missed, so only genuinely new absences move a line. This was measured per player, not assumed.
  • The site is fully generated from the data. Of its 725 table rows, 675 carry a direct link to the official NBA source the values came from. The remaining 50 are design registers — validation gates, improvement proposals, open questions and legal scope — which state no NBA value and therefore cite none. No data row is published without a source.

What is not solid

  • No price. Every ROI assumes −110. A strategy returning +4% at that price may be negative at a real one. This is the single largest defect.
  • Look-ahead contamination. The ratings used in the backtest were captured after the games they predict, so the 78.6% straight-up rate is inflated. It is labelled, not hidden, but it cannot be used.
  • Small sample everywhere. Dispersion from 13 games, totals calibration from 14, injury evidence from 9 verified players out of 88 report rows.
  • Six parlay and prop correlations are unmeasured and left null. Any same-game parlay price would therefore be an assertion, so none is published.
  • The gates are weak at this sample size. A coin-flip control passes three of six. That is documented rather than exploited.
  • 2 absences among 9 verified players are genuinely new (Mobley, Evan, Curry, Stephen), and the assumption governing their impact spans 11 points of total — wider than any plausible edge.

The three decisions this report actually supports

  1. Start a daily pre-tip-off snapshot immediately. It is the only action that simultaneously fixes the sample size, the look-ahead bias and the dispersion estimate, and it costs one scheduled script run per day. See suggestion N01.
  2. Do not publish picks as recommendations until a real price exists. The picks page carries projections, clearly labelled. Converting them into advice without a price would manufacture an edge that has not been measured. See question Q01.
  3. Keep the null control and the contamination label permanently. They are the two instruments that prevent this project from fooling itself later, and both cost nothing to keep. See suggestions N07 and N08.

Honesty ledger

One fabricated row was created during this work and caught by a cross-check: a Darius Garland absence that does not appear on the official report. It was removed and the retraction is recorded in the data file and on the flagged page. Four other defects — an inverted probability, a look-ahead grading method, a rounding-order error and a navigation-depth error — were also found and fixed, each listed with what went wrong. Eight items remain open and are published rather than closed quietly.
