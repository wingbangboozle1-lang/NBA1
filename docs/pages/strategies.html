Grading conventions

Wins pay +100/110 units, losses pay -1 unit. No sportsbook price was observable in this environment, so the standard -110 price is assumed and disclosed rather than presented as observed.

8 strategies were tested in this batch, so every p-value is multiplied by 8 before comparison with 0.05.
