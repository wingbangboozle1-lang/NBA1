Player Props

Per-game projections for every player in the captured official minutes table, shown against their own season average, with each adjustment broken out separately.

!

No probability is published here, on purpose. The model can produce a projection, but game-to-game variance has never been measured — that needs player game logs, which were not captured. Publishing a probability without a measured variance would be inventing a number, so the column is left blank. See F2 and Q3.

What each factor does

Possessions
Expected game possessions ÷ the team's season pace.
Opponent defence
1 + (opponent DefRtg − league average) ÷ league average. Applies to points, assists and threes.
Rebound rate
1 − 0.5 × (opponent DREB% − league average) ÷ league average.
Usage
1 + 0.35 × (teammate minutes unavailable). Only counted when the minutes are known, so it is a floor.
Season avg
The player's own official per-game average. This is the number being projected away from.

The three multipliers are modelling choices, not official measurements — see F4.
