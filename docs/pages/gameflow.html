Reading the probabilities

Within 5
Probability the final home margin lands between −5 and +5 — a one-possession game.
20+
Probability either side wins by 20 or more. Relevant to the NBA Cup, where point differential is a tiebreaker.
P(OT)
Approximate probability the game is tied after regulation, from the density of the margin distribution at zero.
Pace vs league
Expected possessions per 48 minutes minus the league average of 99.85.
