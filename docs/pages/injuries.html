Injuries → rotation → production

The official report says who is out. Turning that into a line movement requires one extra step that is easy to get wrong, and getting it wrong is the difference between a signal and a double-count.

The key correction. Team ratings on NBA.com are season-to-date and already exclude games a player missed. Paolo Banchero has not played since 23 October, so his absence is inside Orlando's rating already. Deducting his 24.5 points again would double-count it. Every player is therefore classified before any adjustment is made, and only NEW_ABSENCE players move a line.

Classification rules

  • NEW_ABSENCE — last appeared 11/10 or later, so team stats do not yet contain a full game without him.
  • ALREADY_BAKED_IN — last appeared 11/09 or earlier, so several team games have already happened without him.
  • SEASON_LONG — no 2026-27 appearances at all.
  • NOT_ABSENT — listed Available or Probable, so no absence is modelled.
