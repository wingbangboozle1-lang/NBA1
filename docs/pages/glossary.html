Glossary

Official definitions where an official one exists, and a clearly separate statement of how this project uses the term.

TermOfficial definitionHow this project uses itSource
PacePossessions per 48 minutes.Used to project a game's possession count as pace_home + pace_away - league_pace.source
POSSPossessions.The denominator for ratings; verified against pace x minutes / 48 in verify_data.py.source
OffRtgOffensive Rating: points scored per 100 possessions.Multiplied by the opponent's DefRtg and divided by the league average to project points.source
DefRtgDefensive Rating: points allowed per 100 possessions.Same as above, in the opponent's direction.source
NetRtgNet Rating: 100 x (points - points allowed) / possessions.Identical to OffRtg - DefRtg. Treated as structural, not a signal.source
TS%True Shooting Percentage: a measure of shooting efficiency that accounts for two- and three-point field goals and free throws.Correlated against OffRtg as a descriptive check.source
eFG%Effective Field Goal Percentage: adjusts a field-goal percentage so that a three-point field goal is worth one and a half field goals.Correlated against OffRtg (r=0.819 across 30 teams).source
TOV%Turnover Percentage: turnovers per 100 plays.Correlated against OffRtg (negative, as expected).source
OREB%Offensive Rebound Percentage: the share of available offensive rebounds a team grabs.Correlated against OffRtg.source
AST%Assist Percentage: the share of a team's field goals that are assisted while a player is on the floor.Tested against pace; no relationship found (r=0.062).source
Originated lineNot an official NBA term.A line this project computes itself from official inputs. Never a sportsbook price.source
Half-lineNot an official NBA term.A prop line set at .5 so that no push is possible.source
NEW_ABSENCENot an official NBA term.A player who last appeared on 11/10 or later, so his absence is not yet inside his team's season stats.source
ALREADY_BAKED_INNot an official NBA term.A player whose absence is already reflected in team form because he missed several prior games.source
Look-ahead biasNot an official NBA term.Using information that was not available before the event being predicted, e.g. season stats that include the game itself.source
Terms that are not official NBA terms. Originated line, half-line, NEW_ABSENCE and ALREADY_BAKED_IN are this project's own vocabulary. They are marked as such in the definition column so they can never be mistaken for league terminology.
