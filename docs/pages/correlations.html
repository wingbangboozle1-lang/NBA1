Model-derived, for parlay pricing

!

These are not measurements. Correlations between bet legs need play-by-play, which was not captured. The values below follow from the structure of the model, not from data. They are labelled MODEL-DERIVED everywhere they appear and must not be quoted as official. The side/total value of 0.00 is a mathematical property of the additive model under equal team-score variances; the player-market values are estimates. See F6.
