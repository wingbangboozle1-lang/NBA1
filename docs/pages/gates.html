Validation gates

Six gates applied as a conjunction. A strategy must pass every one. There is no weighted score, because a weighted score lets a fatal flaw be traded against a cosmetic strength.

How the luck removal actually works

The bootstrap gate resamples the graded outcomes with replacement 10,000 times and asks where the 5th percentile of ROI lands. If a strategy's profit depends on a particular lucky ordering of wins and losses, that percentile falls below zero and the gate fails. This separates a real edge from a favourable sequence.

This gate was degenerate as first written, and it was fixed rather than left flattering. A resampling bootstrap needs losses to resample. With a 7–0 record every resample is all-wins, so the 5th percentile is trivially positive and the gate "passes" a result that contains no information about luck. The gate now requires at least 5 losses before a pass counts, and reports DEGENERATE otherwise. Applying that rule dropped the strategy high-confidence moneyline from 4 gates passed to 3, and the null control from 4 to 3.

The fragility gate is the blunt complement: it deletes the best 5% of winning bets and re-computes ROI. A strategy that only works because one 40-point underdog landed fails here.

Calibration problem, found by the null control. G3 (bootstrap), G4 (stability) and G5 (fragility) are all satisfiable by luck when n is tiny. The NULL CONTROL demonstrates this: a coin-flip strategy passed 4 of 6 gates on these 14 games. The only gates that cannot be gamed at small n are G1 (sample floor) and G2 (Bonferroni-corrected significance), which is precisely why G1 is the first gate. A reader should treat any 'gates passed' count below 6 as meaningless — the conjunction is what matters.

Why the gates were not loosened

Once the null control started passing three of six gates, the tempting move was to relax G3–G5 so the real strategies would look better. That is backwards: the control revealed that those gates are already too permissive at small n, so loosening them further would convert noise into a leaderboard. They were left exactly as they were, and G1 was left first in the chain.
