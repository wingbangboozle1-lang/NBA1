The headline. The single most important number on this site is the graded sample: 14 finished games against a validation floor of 100. Every strategy here fails the sample gate, and the 14 games that do exist are look-ahead contaminated. Rather than tune the gates until something passes, the gates were left alone and the result is reported as a failure.

What was built

Originated linesTotals, spreads and moneylines for all 31 games, computed from offensive/defensive ratings and pace. No sportsbook price is used anywhere, because none was reachable. nba.com
Validation gatesSix gates applied as a conjunction, including a bootstrap luck test, a stability split, and a fragility test that removes the best 5% of bets. gate definitions
CorrelationsTen measured team-level relationships with p-values; six same-game and player-prop correlations deliberately left null because the data to compute them was not reachable. register
Injury → rotation → productionEvery injured player is classified by whether his absence is already inside his team's season stats. Only genuinely new absences move a line. impact
Game flowHow possessions, the 24-second clock and the 14-second reset bound what a game can look like, tied to the actual rule text. flow
Review trailOpen questions, suggestions for improvement, and a flag list that includes an error this project found in its own work and corrected. flagged

The three warnings a reader should carry into every other page

  • No price exists. Every ROI figure assumes a standard −110 price. No sportsbook or exchange quote was observable here, so no edge can be called real.
  • The 14-game backtest is contaminated. The team ratings were captured after the games they predict, which inflates the 78.6% straight-up rate. The page labels it rather than hiding it.
  • The null control passes 3 of 6 gates. A coin-flip strategy clears the bootstrap, stability and fragility gates on 14 games. That is why only the sample floor and the corrected significance test can be trusted at this size — and both fail.
