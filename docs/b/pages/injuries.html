Injuries & Rotation

The official NBA injury report transcribed row by row, plus the rotation impact derived from it. Every status and reason is exactly as the team published it.

!

The impact column is a measured floor, not an estimate. A player's minutes are only known if he appears in the captured official top-50 minutes table. Anyone else contributes 0.00 to the impact, so a team missing a rotation player who is not a top-50 scorer will show a smaller number than the truth — sometimes zero. The Minutes known and Coverage columns sit next to every figure so a 0.00 is never read as "healthy". See F2.

Method

weighted minutes at risk = Σ (status weight × player minutes ÷ 240) impact (net rating points per 100) = 15.0 × weighted minutes at risk

Status weights: Out 1.00 · Doubtful 0.70 · Questionable 0.35 · Probable 0.10 · Available 0.00. The 15.0 coefficient is a declared modelling choice, not an official figure, and a sensitivity band of 10 / 15 / 20 is published for every team on the Flagged page. See F4.
