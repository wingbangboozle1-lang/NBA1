Luck

How much of each team's record is real. Pythagorean expectation applied to the official offensive and defensive ratings, compared against actual wins.

pythag = OffRtg^13.91 ÷ (OffRtg^13.91 + DefRtg^13.91) expected wins = games played × pythag luck (wins) = actual wins − expected wins z = luck ÷ √(games × pythag × (1 − pythag))

The z-score divides the gap by the binomial standard deviation over the games a team has actually played, so it is comparable across teams with different schedules. With 10–13 games played, roughly two thirds of the league should sit inside ±1 — and it does, which is the point: this early, most of the spread in the standings is noise.

What this is used for

A team whose record outruns its efficiency ratings tends to regress. That is a reason to be careful backing them and a reason not to write them off when they are behind their ratings. It is deliberately not wired into the model as an automatic correction: with 10–13 games per team that would be fitting noise. See A4.
