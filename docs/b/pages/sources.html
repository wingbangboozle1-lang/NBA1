Data files

The raw snapshots behind every number are committed to the repository under data/raw/, each with the exact URL and capture time inside it:

  • team_advanced.json — official team advanced stats, season to date and the 2026-10-20…2026-11-05 window
  • games_completed.json — official final scores
  • schedule_upcoming.json — official schedule, venues, tip times
  • injuries.json — the official league injury report, transcribed row by row
  • players_traditional.json — official player per-game averages
  • sources.json — this page, in machine-readable form

Refresh

A scheduled job re-fetches the schedule, results and the newest injury report and commits only when something actually changed. If the job fails, the previously committed snapshot stays live — a failure cannot break the site. See F7.
