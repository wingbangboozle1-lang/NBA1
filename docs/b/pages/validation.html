Information discipline. The test window is 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-13 (60 games — every completed game captured, and all of them after the ratings cut-off). Ratings come only from 2026-10-20 to 2026-11-05, so no test game contributes to the ratings that predict it. Two parameters — home court and the back-to-back penalty — are fitted on the test window; that is disclosed on every row they touch, and a zeroed-parameter variant is published below so you can see how much they matter.
