How parlays are priced

Two-leg parlays show both the probability under the modelled correlation and the probability you would get by naively multiplying the legs. Where they differ, the difference is the amount you would overpay by pricing correlated legs as independent. The correlation values themselves are model-derived, not measured — see Correlations and F6.
