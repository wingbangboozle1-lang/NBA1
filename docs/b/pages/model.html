Model

Exactly how a line is produced, including the parts that are chosen rather than measured.

The core

points per 100 (home) = OffRtg(home) + DefRtg(away) − L points per 100 (away) = OffRtg(away) + DefRtg(home) − L possessions = (Pace(home) + Pace(away)) ÷ 2 margin (home) = (pts100_home − pts100_away) × poss ÷ 100 total = (pts100_home + pts100_away) × poss ÷ 100

L is the league average of the two ratings (112.94 this season). This is the standard additive form.

!

An honest property of this form. Substituting the definitions shows the per-100-possession margin collapses to NetRtg(home) − NetRtg(away): the opponent-interaction term cancels. So the side's forecast carries no opponent-adjustment signal at all beyond the ratings themselves. Home court, rest, availability and shrinkage are the entire game for the side — which is exactly why they are modelled explicitly and reported separately.

Shrinkage

Each team's rating is pulled toward the league average before use:

f = G ÷ (G + k) rating_used = L + f × (rating_observed − L)

k is derived, not guessed: the spread of forecast residuals on the test window implies a single-game team noise of s, and combining that with the observed spread of net ratings gives k = s² ÷ Var(true). The calculation and a fixed-k sensitivity sweep are on the Validation page.

Adjustments

margin += HCA − b2b_penalty × (home_b2b − away_b2b) + (loss_away − loss_home) × poss ÷ 100 total += total_bias + total_b2b_coef × (home_b2b + away_b2b) − (1 − 2α) × (loss_home + loss_away) × poss ÷ 100

α = 0.60 is the share of an availability loss landing on the team's own offence; the rest lands on its defence, so the total moves by −(1 − 2α) × the combined loss. An availability loss is symmetric in the margin by construction — it moves the side only through the difference between the two teams' losses.

What the model does not do

  • No market prices — there is no official source for them, so none is used (F3).
  • No possession-level simulation — no play-by-play was captured.
  • No player-tracking or line-up data — not published in the captured tables.
  • No automatic luck correction in the ratings — with 10–13 games per team that would fit noise. The luck numbers are published separately instead.
  • No inferred values anywhere. Where the official source has nothing, the cell is blank.
